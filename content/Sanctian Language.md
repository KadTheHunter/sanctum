The Sanctian language is the native language of the residents of [[locations/sanctum/index|Sanctum]].

It is a derivative of spoken English, with a morphology based around a triform moral axis, with many words having a Hallowed or Grievant form in addition to their base Neutral form.

## Trivia
- Sanctian was created by [[Kadalyst]] in August 2026
- Sanctian takes inspiration from both [Mando'a](https://mandoa.org/), and [Old English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English)
- The triform register was invented primarily to satisfy the desire for suitably soft and harsh words that could be used in fictional writings pertaining to Sanctum
