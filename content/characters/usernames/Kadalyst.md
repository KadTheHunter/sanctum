Kadalyst is the [[characters/usernames/index.html|username]] of the out-of-universe creator of [[locations/null/sanctum/index|Sanctum]]. He serves as the primary Builder and sole Lore Keeper, steadily expanding both the universe and Minecraft build.

## Contributions & Roles
Kadalyst has designed and built the Sanctum complex in Minecraft, beginning work on 7 January 2025, and moving to [[Odyssey Freedom]] in June 2025.

He often works with [[beccaxoxo]] on the build, collaborating on both the structural design and the interior decor of various rooms.

### Building
The entirety of the Sanctum build can be credited to Kadalyst, as all sections and decor were touched upon and ultimately approved by him. 

### Lore
The majority of the Sanctum lore can be credited to Kadalyst, although some pages and backstories are the creation of other players involved with Sanctum, typically as residents.

## Project Timeline

### Origins (pre-Sanctum)
Prior to creating Sanctum, Kadalyst created two other projects: Scholars[^1], of which few records exist, and the [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/), both of which served as inspiration when creating Sanctum.

### Present
Kadalyst has been working on the Sanctum Build since 7 January 2025, and the Sanctum lore and wiki since June 2026. 

Various other players, including [[beccaxoxo]], [[paige9898]], and [[videogamesm12]] have contributed, both by way of their [[locations/null/sanctum/level-1/personal-rooms/index|Personal Rooms]], and by way of structural design, interior decor, or terrain shaping.
