Kadalyst is the out-of-universe creator of [[locations/sanctum/index|Sanctum]]. He serves as the primary Builder and sole Lore Keeper, steadily expanding both the universe and Minecraft build.

## Contributions & Roles
Kadalyst has designed and built the Sanctum complex in Minecraft, beginning work on 7 January 2025, and moving to [[Odyssey Freedom]] in June 2025.

He often works with [[beccaxoxo]] on the build, collaborating on both the structural design and the interior decor of various rooms.

### Building
The entirety of the Sanctum build can be credited to Kadalyst, as all sections and decor were touched upon and ultimately approved by him. 

### Lore
The majority of the Sanctum lore can be credited to Kadalyst, although some pages and backstories are the creation of other players involved with Sanctum, typically as residents.

## Project Timeline

### Origins (pre-Sanctum)
Prior to creating Sanctum, Kadalyst created two other projects: Scholars, of which few records exist, and the [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/), both of which served as inspiration when creating Sanctum.

#### Scholars
![[ScholarsMain.png|left|350]] Scholars was a Minecraft build very similar to Sanctum, featuring a large mansion set into a snowy mountain, with a wide variety of rooms and levels, including personal rooms, a library, ballroom, kitchens, dining room, recreational rooms, etc. 

Similarly, it also featured a number of bizarre rooms and sub-levels, including Laboratories, Hangars, Portals, and various unfinished sections such as an Underground City, and old or otherwise destroyed sections such as the Hybridian Legion bunker, or Alex's Doll Room.

Scholars was built on Minecraft Bedrock 1.13, which both limited the block palette and added a number of blocks and items that are not usable and do not exist on Minecraft Java, rendering parts of the build broken when ported.

A number of players were involved in its creation besides Kadalyst, some of whom such as [[beccaxoxo]], [[paige9898]], and [[VentralArt38119]] have joined Sanctum as well.

#### Kædykos Nebula

The Kædykos Nebula was a lore project expanding on previously unpublished ideas regarding the [[Neurax Clones]], adding in a larger universe involving characters such as [[Kaedykos]], and locations such as [[Vesper Prime]], which have since been carried on to Sanctum as well.

### Present
Kadalyst has been working on the Sanctum Build since 7 January 2025, and the Sanctum lore and wiki since June 2026. 

Various other players, including [[beccaxoxo]], [[paige9898]], and [[videogamesm12]] have contributed, both by way of their [[locations/sanctum/level 1/personal rooms/index|Personal Rooms]], and by way of structural design, interior decor, or terrain shaping.
