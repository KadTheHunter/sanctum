beccaxoxo is a major contributor to the Minecraft build of Sanctum, particularly the interior decor, and has assisted in the design of much of Sanctum's structure, as well as its mountainous exterior.

## Contributions & Roles
Becca has contributed to nearly every room in Sanctum except for residents [[locations/sanctum/level 1/personal rooms/|Personal Rooms]].

### Interior Decor
The majority of Becca's contributions consist of interior design and decor, most notably on the following rooms:
- [[Great Hall]]
	- Upper Balconies
- [[Living Room]] 
	- Renovation, much of the furniture and decor
- [[Small Dining Room]] 
	- Table and decor
- [[Main Library]] 
	- Design and some decor
- [[Upside-Down House]] 
	- House interior, some terrain

### Other Contributions
She has also contributed by way of the standard corridor design, which has seen use across the [[locations/sanctum/level 0/|Ground Floor]], and [[locations/sanctum/level 1/|Level 1]]
  
## Project Timeline

### Origins (pre-Sanctum)
Prior to her work on Sanctum, Becca was involved with [[Kadalyst|Kadalyst's]] work on Scholars[^1], a project generally considered to be Sanctum's predecessor.

Her contributions were much the same as with Sanctum, helping to expand and decorate various rooms and hallways.

### Present
Becca was involved with Sanctum as early as 7 January 2025, but did not have an opportunity to contribute until it was moved to [[Odyssey Freedom]] in June 2025.

Since then, she's made regular contributions, working to expand the build one room at a time.
