Welcome to the Sanctum Wiki! 

This wiki collects and organizes all the information for [[Kadalyst|Kadalyst's]] Sanctum universe, including both the Minecraft build and the lore.

# About
Sanctum is a cross-format worldbuilding project spanning a Minecraft server and the wiki you are reading. It began as bits of funny, whimsical, or outright absurd lore invented to justify or explain details of a Minecraft build. Over time, they slowly expanded into the full lore and canon recorded here.

The build and the lore draw inspiration from a wide variety of media. Major influences include: *[Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars)*, *[The Checquy Files](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rook_(novel))*, *[The 100 Cupboards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_Cupboards)*, *[Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter)*, *[Ender's Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ender's_Game_(novel_series))*, and *[The 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_100_(TV_series))*.

## Project Timeline
Prior to creating Sanctum, [[Kadalyst]] created two other projects: Scholars (of which few records exist) and [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/), both of which served as inspiration when creating Sanctum.

### Scholars (2018-2020)
![[ScholarsMain.png|left|350]]
*Scholars* was a Minecraft build very similar to Sanctum, featuring a large mansion set into a snowy mountain with a wide variety of rooms and levels. These included personal rooms, a library, a ballroom, kitchens, a dining room, and recreational rooms. 

Similarly, it also featured a number of bizarre rooms and sub-levels, including laboratories, hangars, portals, and various unfinished sections such as an underground city. It also contained abandoned or destroyed sections, such as the Hybridian Legion bunker or Alex's Doll Room.

Scholars was built on Minecraft Bedrock 1.13, which both limited the block palette and added a number of blocks and items that are not usable and do not exist on Minecraft Java, rendering parts of the build broken when ported.

A number of other players were involved in its creation besides Kadalyst. Some of them, such as [[beccaxoxo]], [[paige9898]], and [[VentralArt38119]] have since joined Sanctum as well.

### Kædykos Nebula (2022-2023)

The [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/) was a lore project expanding on unpublished ideas regarding the [[characters/neurax-clones/index|Neurax Clones]]. It added a larger universe involving characters such as [[Kaedykos Nightingale]], and locations such as [[Vesper Prime]], which have since been carried on to Sanctum.

### Present
Kadalyst has been working on the Sanctum build since 7 January 2025, and the Sanctum lore and wiki since June 2026. 

Various other players, including beccaxoxo, paige9898, and videogamesm12 have contributed, both by way of their [[locations/null/sanctum/level-1/personal-rooms/index|Personal Rooms]], and by way of structural design, interior decor, and terrain shaping.
