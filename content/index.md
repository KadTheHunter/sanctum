## Project Timeline
Prior to creating Sanctum, [[Kadalyst]] created two other projects: [[#Scholars]], of which few records exist, and the [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/), both of which served as inspiration when creating Sanctum.

### Scholars
![[ScholarsMain.png|left|350]] Scholars was a Minecraft build very similar to Sanctum, featuring a large mansion set into a snowy mountain, with a wide variety of rooms and levels, including personal rooms, a library, ballroom, kitchens, dining room, recreational rooms, etc. 

Similarly, it also featured a number of bizarre rooms and sub-levels, including Laboratories, Hangars, Portals, and various unfinished sections such as an Underground City, and old or otherwise destroyed sections such as the Hybridian Legion bunker, or Alex's Doll Room.

Scholars was built on Minecraft Bedrock 1.13, which both limited the block palette and added a number of blocks and items that are not usable and do not exist on Minecraft Java, rendering parts of the build broken when ported.

A number of players were involved in its creation besides Kadalyst, some of whom such as [[beccaxoxo]], [[paige9898]], and [[VentralArt38119]] have joined Sanctum as well.

### Kædykos Nebula

The [Kædykos Nebula](https://kadthehunter.github.io/Kydykos-Nebula/) was a lore project expanding on previously unpublished ideas regarding the [[characters/neurax-clones/index|Neurax Clones]], adding in a larger universe involving characters such as [[Kaedykos Nightingale]], and locations such as [[Vesper Prime]], which have since been carried on to Sanctum as well.

### Present
Kadalyst has been working on the Sanctum Build since 7 January 2025, and the Sanctum lore and wiki since June 2026. 

Various other players, including beccaxoxo, paige9898, and videogamesm12 have contributed, both by way of their [[locations/null/sanctum/level-1/personal-rooms/index|Personal Room]], and by way of structural design, interior decor, or terrain shaping.
