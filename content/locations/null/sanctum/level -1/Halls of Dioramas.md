The Halls of Dioramas are a series of hallways in [[locations/null/sanctum/level--1/index|The Cellar]] containing dioramas of various scenes, both historical and fictitious.

Notable scenes include the [[#Harry Potter vs Lord Voldemort]] diorama.

## Harry Potter vs Lord Voldemort
![[HarryPottervsLordVoldemort.png]] The Harry Potter vs Lord Voldemort diorama depicts the final duel between Harry and Voldemort in the Hogwarts courtyard, both casting their iconic Expelliarmus and Avada Kedavra spells respectively.
