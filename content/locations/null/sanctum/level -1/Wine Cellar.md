The Wine Cellar is one of several storage rooms in [[locations/null/sanctum/level--1/index|The Cellar]], and contains an array of 12 large casks of wine, ale and mead. 

Barrels are brought in and out via a special tunnel built into the back, that leads to the [[Hangar]] and [[Storage Vault]].

Commonly stocked casks include a dry Zarconian Red, aged 20 years, a nutty Vesperian White, aged 10 years, as well as Honey Mead, and the locally produced Isolan Ale.
