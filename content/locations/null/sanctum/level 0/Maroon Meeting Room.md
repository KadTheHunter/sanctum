![[MaroonMeetingRoom1.png|left|350]] The Maroon Meeting Room is a small boardroom located to the left of the [[Main Library]] on [[locations/null/sanctum/level-1/index|Level 1]]. It is typically used for private or informal meetings of [[locations/null/sanctum/index|Sanctum]] members, [[characters/nightingales/index|Nightingales]] in particular.

The walls are kept blank in preparation for signs, whiteboards, blackboards, nullboards, printouts, cutouts and other hang-able objects. 

Besides an intricately carved table made from [[Isola|Isolan]] stone, and imported [[Wyyrwood]] chairs, the meeting room's sole other piece of furniture is an old wooden cabinet located on the back right panel. Various members have failed to remove it despite their best efforts; It appears to be quite literally rooted into the wood of the floor, and possessed of an alarmingly powerful and unwavering strength.

## History
The Maroon Meeting Room is one of the oldest builds in Sanctum, predated only by the [[Great Hall]], and certain parts of [[locations/null/sanctum/level--1/index|The Cellar]].

Following the construction of the Great Hall, the meeting room was designed by [[Bela Nightingale]] as a hub for the ongoing construction of Sanctum, with the walls and table being festooned with blueprints and designs. 

After the majority of Sanctum's major rooms had been completed, the meeting room was cleared out and repurposed into its current form, as a private or informal meeting room exclusive to members.
