![[CandyOdayakasRoom1.png|left|350]] CandyOdayaka's Room is an old bedroom hidden inside [[locations/null/sanctum/level-0a/index|Level 0.5]], accessible via the upper floor of the [[Main Library]] or [[The Signrooms]].

The original doorway is hidden behind a false bookshelf in the Main Library, and the wall between the windows conceals a passageway into The Signrooms; The windows themselves have been blacked out, though it's unknown why they ever existed, given the room is buried deep within the mountainous terrain of [[Isola]].

## Trivia
- Originally built in 2018, it was ported from Bedrock Edition to Java Edition as part of a larger export of builds and maps from XOXO High RolePlay
- It was built and occupied by CandyOdayaka in the Scholars[^1] mansion, and is one of the few surviving personal rooms from then.
